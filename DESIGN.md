# SPELLDROP design specification

## Brand character

SPELLDROP is a modern magical delivery service.

Personality:
- playful
- competent
- slightly absurd
- friendly
- not childish
- not medieval

## Core visual principle

The UI is restrained.
The illustrations provide most of the color and personality.

## Background

Use one continuous gradient across the page:

- deep navy-purple at the top
- subtle muted violet in the middle
- dark warm brown near the bottom

No photographic texture.
No scenery objects.
No background shelves.
No stars covering the full page.

## Card styling

Default card:
- dark tinted surface
- thin low-contrast border
- 24px radius
- no strong outer glow

Selected card:
- lime border
- subtle lime shadow
- white checkmark
- no scale larger than 1.015

## Mascot usage

The witch mascot appears:
- as the hero character
- attached to the summary panel
- in the success state

Do not place the mascot in every card.
Do not attach it directly to the viewport edge.

## Color coding

Situation:
- Lost item: green
- Inspiration: violet
- Awkward pause: blue
- Dragon: orange/red
- Plant: olive
- Other: turquoise

Effects:
- Fast: lime
- Long-lasting: violet
- Safe: blue
- Glow: orange
- Silent: cyan
- Aroma: pink

Delivery:
- Owl: muted violet
- Express: warm orange/violet
- Teleport: electric violet
